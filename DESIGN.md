# Rakshak AI design system

Farmers use phones in bright daylight while inspecting crops; experts review cases on office monitors. Use light, high-contrast surfaces with restrained green actions and explicit amber/red status labels.

## Color

Canvas: warm pale neutral. Surfaces: nearly white, subtly green tinted. Text: deep charcoal green. Primary action: dark forest green. Secondary surfaces: muted sage. Warning: amber with dark text. Error: dark red on pale rose. Never communicate a result with color alone.

## Typography

Use a system sans family. Body text 16px, supporting labels at least 13px, headings 24–32px in product views. Keep farmer explanations short and within 70 characters per line. Expert tables may use denser layouts.

## Layout and components

Mobile uses a single task column and Fields / History / Profile navigation. Desktop uses a persistent navigation rail with a clear page title and primary action. Use bordered evidence panels and tables when they aid comparison. Avoid nested cards and decorative metric panels.

Controls have consistent heights, labels, focus rings and busy states. Touch targets are at least 44px. Forms validate inline and retain input on failure. Empty fields lead to field creation; empty scan lists lead to capture. Product transitions convey state and respect reduced motion.
