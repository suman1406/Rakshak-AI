# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mobile is the primary farmer experience. Web prioritizes organizations, agronomists and administrators. Farmers record soybean fields outdoors on phones; agronomists reviewing visual evidence; organizations monitoring their own farms. Farmers need a short, legible path from field selection to capture, results and expert review.

## Product Purpose

Implement the soybean video intelligence MVP defined in docs/source-plans/Fasal_Rakshak_AI_PRD.md. Assess multiple observations and explain uncertainty. An AI indication is not a confirmed diagnosis.

## Brand Personality

Practical, clear, trustworthy. The user approved an accessible field tool with denser expert evidence views on 9 September 2026.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, fabricated metrics, internal API instructions in farmer journeys, and unsupported diagnostic certainty.

## Design Principles

- Put the next useful action first.
- Keep evidence and uncertainty together.
- Preserve familiar navigation and consistent controls.
- Make empty, failed and offline states actionable.
- Separate simulated examples from personal records.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable outdoor phone use, and text labels alongside semantic colors.
